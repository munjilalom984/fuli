1. Introduction

Welcome to TagNotes ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our services.

By downloading, installing, or using TagNotes, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide and synchronize your notebook across your devices:

A. Account Information

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in using Google Identity Services (Credential Manager), we receive your Google display name, email address, profile picture URL, and a unique Google user ID.
  • Email & Password Sign-In: If you register using your email and password, we store your email address and an authenticated user ID managed securely via Firebase Authentication. We never store or have access to your plain-text password.

B. User-Generated Content (Notes & Tags)

  • Note Data: Titles, text body, custom tags (e.g., #work, #personal), color selections, pinned states, archive states, and creation/modification timestamps.
  • Your notes are associated directly with your unique user ID and are stored securely on Google Cloud Firestore.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information

  • Basic device telemetry, operating system version, and error logs collected automatically by Google Play Services and Firebase SDKs to troubleshoot crashes and improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: Verifying your identity and keeping you safely signed in.
  • Cross-Device Synchronization: Storing and synchronizing your notes and tag taxonomy across multiple Android devices in real time.
  • Personalized Organization: Enabling quick tag filtering, search, and category grouping.
  • App Reliability: Diagnosing errors, preventing security abuse, and ensuring system availability.
No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or notes with third parties or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

TagNotes utilizes reputable, enterprise-grade cloud services provided by Google LLC to deliver authentication and real-time database capabilities:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages identity authentication for Google Sign-In and Email/Password accounts.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Provides persistent cloud storage and real-time data sync with zero-trust database security rules.
  • Google Play Services: Powers credential management and identity tokens.

For further details on how Google processes and secures data, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encrypted Transmission: All communications between the app and cloud servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Zero-Trust Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules strictly mandate that only the authenticated user matching the account UID can read, write, or delete notes in their personal directory (users/{userId}/notes).
  • Restricted Access: No other user of TagNotes can view, query, or edit your notes.

6. Data Retention, User Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Deleting Individual Notes: You can edit or permanently delete any note directly inside the app at any time. When deleted, it is immediately removed from Cloud Firestore.
  • Account & Complete Data Deletion: If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated notes permanently, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us at munjilalom984@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - TagNotes". Your account and all stored records will be purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

TagNotes is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.
